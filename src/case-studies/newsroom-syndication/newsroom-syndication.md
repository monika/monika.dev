## The challenge

While setting up [sitewide search](/case-studies/site-search/), I found that the newsroom couldn't be indexed. News items had no URL or detail page of their own, so there was nothing for search to index and nothing for results to point to. Every news click also sent the reader to another site.

It was an extension of the [GEO/AEO pilot](/case-studies/geo-aeo-authority-pilot/) waiting to happen. That work was about proving our content came from real people with real expertise, and the newsroom was a section of the site where we couldn't do that at all. Every story was a missed chance to build traffic, and to attach our disambiguated author to more pages and build even more credibility.

The newsroom was also managed and maintained by our brand director, so any fix had to work for readers and be easy for them to run.

## The approach

**Syndicate it, credit the source.** I created a page on our site for each story, using the content from the links we'd been sending readers to. A canonical tag on each page credits the original source. We weren't taking credit for the content, only letting it work for us from inside the CMS.

**Attach the authors.** Because every story now had its own page, I could pair it with the SME program from the GEO/AEO pilot. Each page systematically generates schema that references our subject matter experts, the same disambiguated authors, so the credibility we'd built carried over to news instead of stopping at the blog.

**Keep it in the brand director's hands.** The stories live in the CMS where the brand director already worked, so adding a story doesn't need a developer.

## The result

* **Readers stay on our site** when they open a news story.
* **News is part of sitewide search**, so we can now see which kinds of news visitors interact with.
* **More pages carry our authors.** Each story is another page where the disambiguated author's credentials reach machines, strengthening our entities.
* **The brand director can manage it** in the CMS.
